What the simulator computes

Each stone is a planar rigid body carrying a body-fixed dipole moment. The far-field pair interaction is

Uij = C r−3[mi·mj − 3(mi·r̂)(mj·r̂)].

Force scales as r−4, torque as r−3. Motion begins when the reduced force–torque wrench reaches the Coulomb-like static limit:

χ = √[(‖F‖/μsN)² + (τ/μsNρ)²] ≥ 1.

Edge-supported stones have a separate tipping guard. Contact is compliant; sufficiently attractive, low-energy contacts become capture joints. Those discrete graph changes make the dynamics hybrid and allow cascades.

Interpretation

The app is a dimensionless, reduced explanatory simulator. It implements the theoretical architecture of the paper but is not yet calibrated to a particular manufactured set. Near-contact forces are softened and capped because the point-dipole approximation is not quantitatively valid there.

Risk planner

The heat map evaluates the maximum predicted friction/tipping utilization produced by placing the held dipole at each candidate point. “Safe” minimizes predicted hazard and rewards clearance. “Trap” searches below the instability threshold for high-susceptibility placements.
